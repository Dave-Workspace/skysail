Understanding replacement of a PR Card

Factors to Consider

Permanent residents of Canada must ensure their PR card remains valid, as it serves as proof of status and is required for re-entry into the country. When applying for renewal or replacement, applicants should confirm they meet the residency obligation—living in Canada for at least 730 days in the past five years. Applications must include accurate personal details, updated photographs, and any supporting documents to avoid delays. If the card is lost, stolen, or damaged, a clear explanation and relevant evidence are required. Applying early before the card expires is important to prevent travel interruptions.

Eligibility Criteria

Permanent residents of Canada are eligible to renew or replace their PR card if it has expired, is about to expire, or has been lost, stolen, or damaged. Applicants must maintain permanent resident status, meet the residency requirement, and provide valid identification documents. They should also demonstrate compliance with Canadian laws and not be under a removal order. Eligibility depends on proving both continued residency in Canada and the need for a new or renewed PR card.
